What this site does not do

This is a static website. It sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, embeds no social media widgets, has no contact form, no newsletter and no user accounts. We do not collect, store or process any personal data ourselves.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, browser and operating system. GitHub processes this to deliver the page and to keep the service secure and stable. We have no access to these logs.

GitHub is based in the United States. Its privacy statement is at docs.github.com, and the GitHub Pages data protection notes are at docs.github.com/pages.

Legal basis

Processing of this technical data rests on our legitimate interest in providing a functioning website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. When you click one, you leave this website. The partner — for example Tiqets, Kiwitaxi, GetRentacar, Localrent, Klook, Yesim, EKTA, Aviasales or AirHelp, with tracking handled by Travelpayouts — may set a cookie in your browser or otherwise record that you arrived through us, so that a booking can be attributed to us.

That processing happens on the partner's side and under the partner's privacy policy, not ours. We receive only aggregated statistics — how many clicks and bookings a link produced — never your name, your email address or your booking details.

If you do not want this, do not click the affiliate links; the rest of the site works exactly the same without them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information about your stored data, to correction, to erasure, to restriction of processing, to data portability, and to object to processing. You may also complain to a supervisory authority.

Because we store no personal data ourselves, requests about data collected while delivering this site have to be directed to GitHub as the host, or to the respective partner for data collected after you follow an affiliate link.

Changes

If the site gains functions that process data — analytics, a contact form, a newsletter — this page will be updated before those functions go live.
