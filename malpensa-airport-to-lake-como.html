Lake Como

Malpensa Airport to Varenna

About 107 kilometres to the one village on Lake Como where the railway and the car ferry meet. Usually a train journey, sometimes not.

The short version

Route at a glance

  • Route: Milan Malpensa (MXP) → Varenna
  • Distance: about 107 km
  • Driving time: roughly 1 hour 45
  • Price from: around 335 USD per vehicle, not per person

This is the one route on this site where we would tell most people to take the train.

The train is genuinely good here

Varenna has its own station, Varenna-Esino, on the Milan–Lecco–Tirano line, and the walk from the platform down into the village is short. From Malpensa it is the Malpensa Express into Milan, then one train out along the eastern shore — one change, both legs frequent, and the second half of the ride runs right beside the lake.

For two people travelling light, that is cheaper than a transfer by a wide margin and not meaningfully slower once you count check-in-free boarding.

When the transfer still wins

  • A late flight. The connection thins out in the evening and a missed last train on the Lecco line is an expensive taxi.
  • Luggage. Milano Centrale with three large cases and a change of platform is the part of the holiday nobody enjoys.
  • Four or more of you. Per vehicle rather than per ticket, and the arithmetic flips.
  • Staying across the water. If your hotel is in Bellagio or Menaggio you have a ferry to catch afterwards either way — arriving relaxed and on time matters more.

Booking the practical bits

  • Private transfer Malpensa → Varenna — fixed price, driver at arrivals, 90 minutes of free waiting.
  • An eSIM for Italy — ferry timetables change with the season and are easiest to check on your phone at the pier.
  • If the flight is delayed or cancelled.

Why Varenna rather than Como town

Como is a city with a lake attached — useful, well connected, not what most people are picturing. Varenna is the village on the postcard, and it happens to sit at the one point on the lake where the railway and the car ferry meet. From there Bellagio and Menaggio are a short crossing rather than an hour of lakeside road.
