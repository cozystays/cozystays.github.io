Verona Airport to Val Gardena

About 192 kilometres to Selva, a little less to Ortisei, and no railway station anywhere in the valley. What each way in involves.

The short version

Route at a glance

  • Route: Verona Villafranca Airport (VRN) → Selva di Val Gardena
  • Distance: about 192 km
  • Driving time: roughly 2.5 to 3 hours
  • Price from: around 375 USD per vehicle, not per person

Ortisei is the first of the three villages you reach and a few kilometres shorter; Santa Cristina sits between them. The difference in driving time across the valley is minutes, not hours.

Which village do you actually want?

Give the driver the right one, because the three are not interchangeable and walking between them with luggage is not a plan.

  • Ortisei (about 1,236 m) — the largest, a real town with a pedestrian centre, the weakest lift connection of the three.
  • Santa Cristina (about 1,428 m) — quieter, better placed for the lifts, usually the cheapest.
  • Selva (about 1,563 m) — where the Sella Ronda starts and where the skiing is the point.

The longer version of this comparison is on our Val Gardena page.

How else you can get there

Train plus bus

There is no station in Val Gardena. The railway gets you to Bolzano or Brixen, and from there it is roughly an hour by bus up the valley. From Verona that means a train north to Bolzano and then the bus — perfectly doable in daylight with one bag each, considerably less so at 21:00 in February with skis.

Hire car

Less useful here than almost anywhere else in Italy. The lifts connect the three villages, which is precisely why so few visitors bother with a car once they arrive. In winter it is mostly a thing to park.

Private transfer

Door to door, one fixed price for the vehicle, ski bags included rather than wrestled onto a bus, and the driver tracks the flight.

When it is not worth it

Two people, hand luggage, a midday arrival, no ski gear: take the train to Bolzano and the bus up. It is cheaper, it is scenic, and nothing about it is difficult.

Everything else — a family, a group, equipment, an evening landing — tips the other way, and the per-vehicle pricing is why.

Booking the practical bits

  • Private transfer Verona airport → Selva di Val Gardena — fixed price, driver at arrivals, 90 minutes of free waiting.
  • Private transfer Verona airport → Ortisei — same road, the first village in the valley.
  • An eSIM for Italy — South Tyrol is close enough to the border that phones sometimes wander onto an Austrian network.
  • If the flight is delayed or cancelled.

Arriving in winter

Saturday is changeover day across the whole region and the valley roads queue for it — an hour or more on top in peak weeks. The last stretch up from the Isarco valley is mountain road; in snow, the two and a half hours become three without anything going wrong.
