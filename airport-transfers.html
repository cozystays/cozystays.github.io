When a private transfer is worth it

A transfer is priced per vehicle, not per person. That single fact decides most of these cases.

  • Three or more people. Split four ways, almost every route below becomes competitive with individual train tickets.
  • Ski bags, a pram, or more than one large suitcase each. Changing trains with them is the part nobody photographs.
  • A late arrival. Mountain buses stop early. A flight landing at 21:00 at Verona does not connect to anything going up the valley.
  • No station at the destination. Cortina and Val Gardena have none; Livigno is a long way from one.

And when it is not worth it: two people travelling light to a town with a direct train. Varenna and Rome are exactly that case, and we say so on those pages.

What is included

On the routes we link, the fixed price covers a private car for your party, a driver meeting you inside the terminal with a nameplate, and 90 minutes of free waiting after the scheduled arrival time — which matters more than it sounds in winter, when a delayed flight otherwise means a missed connection. Extra stops, a different vehicle class or waiting beyond that window can cost more.

Prices on this site are approximate and move with season and demand. Check the current figure before you book.
