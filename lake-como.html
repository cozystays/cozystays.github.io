Region guide

Lake Como

An upside-down Y of deep water between mountains, with villages on every shore. The good news for visitors: the ferries make the shape irrelevant.

Which village to stay in

Stay in the middle of the lake, where the three main villages face each other and the ferries run most often. The city of Como, at the southern tip, is convenient but has little of what people come for.

Varenna, for charm and the train

Small, steep, orange and pink, with a lakeside walkway pinned to the rock. It is the only one of the three with a railway station, which makes arriving from Milan straightforward. Quieter in the evening than Bellagio.

Bellagio, for the setting

On the point where the lake splits, which is why it appears on every cover. Stepped alleys, expensive shops, and a great many day visitors between eleven and five. Lovely once they leave.

Menaggio, for value

The most ordinary of the three, and the cheapest. Flat, with a proper town square, a good base if you want to walk or cycle and do not need postcard views from your window.

Getting there and getting around

From Milan

Malpensa is the usual airport. From Milan Centrale, trains run to Varenna in about an hour — the cheapest way in if you are travelling light. From the airport itself, a direct transfer saves the airport-to-city leg.

Use the ferries, not the road

Varenna, Bellagio and Menaggio sit on different shores and are linked by a frequent car-and-passenger ferry that takes about fifteen minutes. Driving between them means going the long way round on a narrow lakeside road. Longer boats run the length of the lake and are worth taking once just for the villa fronts, most of which were designed to be seen from the water.

A car is optional at best

Parking in the villages is limited and often paid, and the roads are narrow and busy in season. Unless you plan to leave the lake, you will do better without one.

Booking the practical bits

  • Private transfer Bergamo airport → Lake Como — about 1 h and 94 km. Most cheap flights to the lake land at Bergamo, not Milan, and the public route is a shuttle bus, two trains and a ferry.
  • Private transfer Malpensa → Varenna — fixed price, straight to the lake, no change in Milan.
  • Lake Como boat tours and cruises — the villa fronts and the middle of the lake from the water.

Worth your time

Villa del Balbianello near Lenno, on a wooded promontory with terraces over the water. Arrive by boat if you can; the walk in is pleasant but the approach from the lake is the point.

Villa Carlotta and Villa Melzi for gardens — best in spring when the azaleas and rhododendrons are out.

The greenway above Menaggio, an easy marked path through old villages, for an afternoon on foot without a serious climb.

Stay for the evening ferry. The last crossings, after the day visitors have gone, are the quietest and best time on the water.

Before you go

Ferry timetables change with the season and are worth checking the night before rather than assuming — an eSIM makes that easy. Travel cover rounds out the pre-flight list.
